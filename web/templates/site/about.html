About

Hi, I'm Adder Oaks!

I live in central Virginia, where I've been teaching math to students of all ages for over fifteen years. For quite a while, I have been searching for the perfect calculus course for my students. There are many great resources out there, but none that quite match my teaching style, focused on visual conceptual introductions with rigorous practice problems to reinforce core ideas. I decided to make such a course myself and share it with the world. I hope this is just the course you were looking for!

A note about AI

I used generative artificial intelligence heavily for the technical aspects of producing this course. I made sure to produce a series of lessons according to my personal teaching style and expertise as a calculus teacher. The website engineering, animation, and individual problem sets were developed by AI, subject to my review. If you have any questions about my use of AI, don't hesitate to reach out.
