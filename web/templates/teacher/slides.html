Presenting

  • Next slide: →, Page Down or Space (most clickers send these). Back: ← or Page Up.
  • F goes fullscreen, T switches between light and dark, K plays or pauses the clip, B blacks the screen.
  • The deck opens in the look you picked for the site. Light usually reads better on a projector.

PowerPoint and Google Slides

The PowerPoint file has the same slides. Titles are editable text, the math is a picture rendered at full HD, and each slide's speaker notes hold its text (in the solutions version, the worked solution too). Clips are embedded and play in PowerPoint. Google Slides can't play video embedded in a PowerPoint file, so after you import it there the clips show as still frames.
